## 1. Plant–Soil–Microbial Ecology and Applications

This research examines plant–soil–microbial interactions in surface ecosystems, linking microbial community structure and environmental drivers to ecosystem responses. It focuses on how climate, water availability, and land use shape these interactions and influence ecosystem function and resilience. In earlier work, I mainly focused on carbon cycling and greenhouse gas fluxes, particularly CO₂ and CH₄. More recently, I have also explored more application-oriented work using microbiome approaches in environmental and agricultural systems.

## 2. Anaerobic Redox Biogeochemistry and Environmental Contaminants

This research investigates biochemical processes in anaerobic and flooded environments, focusing on methane cycling, redox transformations, and microbial degradation of environmental pollutants. It emphasizes how microbial metabolism drives both carbon flow and contaminant fate in natural and impacted systems.

## 3. Microbial Evolution and Predictive Microbiome Systems (Open Area)

This research focuses on synthetic microbial systems to understand evolutionary processes, microbial interactions, and multi-level mechanisms independent of environmental complexity. It integrates predictive modeling, genome-resolved microbiomics, and high-throughput sequencing to investigate bio-info across both simplified and environmental contexts, with ongoing development of methods and applications.
